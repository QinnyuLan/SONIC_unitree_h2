The generated model preserves the Unitree MuJoCo model's kinematics, visual and
collision geometry, actuators, and sensors. Each body's mass, center of mass,
principal-axis orientation, and principal inertia are replaced by values from
the Unitree ROS URDF after MuJoCo fixed-joint folding. This folds the URDF hand
inertials into their corresponding wrist-yaw bodies without importing the ROS
collision meshes.

Regenerate with:

  python3 scripts/build_h2_unitree_mujoco_ros_mass.py
